# Call for photos (draft, ready to post)

For the BAND app and email. Edit freely; the list of what we need comes from `todo.md` (Photos, New headshots).

---

**📸 Help us show off the band! Photos wanted for our new website**

We're building a new website for the Portuguese Band of San Leandro, and we need your photos: rehearsals, parades, concerts, dinners, lessons, behind the scenes. Recent photos are best, but older and historical ones are welcome too.

**Especially wanted:**
- 🍽️ A **fundraiser dinner**, or the band performing for the community, with people smiling
- 🎓 **Students in a lesson** with Edgard, or young and older musicians side by side
- 🏠 **Our clubhouse** at 844 Alvarado St., from the street, in daylight
- 🎺 A **concert or festival**, the band in action
- 🎶 A current **Friday rehearsal**
- 📜 **Old photos** from our early years (1980s and 90s), even prints you can photograph

**Tips:**
- **Landscape (sideways)** photos work best, from a phone or camera.
- Send the **original file**, not a screenshot or a photo saved from social media.
- Action and faces are great; blurry or dark photos are hard to use.

**How to send:** upload them to our shared folder: **[link]** _(or post them in the BAND album "Website photos")_

**Please only share photos you took or have permission to share.** By sending them you let the band use them on our website and printed materials; tell us if you'd like a photo credit. We only publish photos of students whose families signed our photo consent form.

Thank you! 🎵

---

## Notes for whoever collects them

- **Upload folder:** a "Website photos (inbox)" folder in the band's Google Drive.
  - A public folder that anyone can **add** to also lets anyone **delete**. Safer: share it with band members' accounts, or use a Google Form with a file upload question, which collects into a Drive folder only the owner manages.
- **From BAND:** download the album photos on a computer (band.us → the album → download) and add them to the Drive folder.
- **Then:** the site build only uses photos copied into the repo (`src/assets/photos/`), and only the chosen ones. See `todo.md` for how the files get from Drive to the repo.
- **Minors:** only students with a signed consent form, and no full names on photos of minors (`brief.md` §5.1).
